Purpose Path Brand Guideline


1. Brand Identity

Company Name: Purpose Path
Mission: To transform businesses into engaged, values-driven organizations where leaders and teams are aligned, motivated, and empowered to turn vision into reality.
Target Audience: Business owners and CEOs of small to midsize companies.
Brand Tone & Voice: Professional, confident, and inspiring. Your communication should be clear, authoritative, and solutions-oriented. It should convey a sense of expertise and a focus on long-term growth and success.

2. Logo Usage

Primary Logo: The attached circular logo is the primary brand mark. It should be used consistently across all materials.

3. Color Palette

The colors you've chosen are excellent for conveying professionalism and innovation. I've added a few supporting neutrals to create a complete palette.
Primary Palette
Dark Blue
Hex: #192c64
Used for: Headlines, body text, and primary design elements. It's a color that signifies trust and authority.
Turquoise
Hex: #3ac2b5
Used for: Accents, call-to-action buttons, and to highlight key information. This color represents growth and innovation.
Golden Yellow
Hex: #caa356
Used for: Special accents or to add a touch of sophistication. This color evokes ideas of quality and value.
Supporting Neutral Palette
Charcoal Gray
Hex: #4f5257
Used for: Body text and secondary headings for improved readability on light backgrounds.
Light Gray
Hex: #f5f5f5
Used for: Backgrounds and borders. It provides a clean, modern feel that allows the primary colors to stand out.

4. Typography

To appeal to your target audience of business leaders, I recommend fonts that are clean, professional, and easy to read.
Primary Font (Headings): Roboto Slab
A bold and modern font that makes a strong statement. It works well for headlines and titles.
Secondary Font (Body Text): Open Sans
A clean, highly readable font that is perfect for body paragraphs, subheadings, and captions. It's professional and accessible.
Logo font - the closest and most available (free) is Montserrat ExtraBold
